Privacy Policy

For iOS and iPadOS. Effective October 18, 2026. Version 1.1.

English简体中文Support

Quasar is a client for personal media servers. It works with Emby, Jellyfin and Feiniu (飞牛影视) servers that you run yourself or have an account on.

This policy covers the Quasar app for iOS and iPadOS. The Android app is distributed separately and has additional features.

Quasar has no account system and no developer-operated content service. We do not receive your media server password. We do not run analytics or advertising.

Information kept on your device

  • Media server address, username and password. Quasar stores them in the iOS Keychain. It sends them only to the server address you entered.
  • A random device ID. Quasar creates it on first launch and sends it to your media server so the server can recognize this client. It is not an advertising identifier and is not shared with us.
  • Settings, watch progress, library metadata, artwork and playback cache.

On iOS, Keychain items can remain after you delete the app. Delete the app to remove local data. Keychain items may outlive it as described.

Information sent over the network

Quasar sends requests directly from your device. We do not proxy them.

  • Your media server. Requests include your credentials, device ID, library queries, playback progress and watch state. The person or company that operates the server handles that data under their own privacy policy.
  • TMDB (themoviedb.org). Title searches and metadata. TMDB receives your IP address. Quasar uses a shared API credential, so you do not need a TMDB account.
  • Trakt (trakt.tv). Public trending lists and artwork for the Discover and detail screens, and air times for the Calendar screen. Trakt receives your IP address. This version of the iOS app does not sign in to Trakt and does not send your watch history to Trakt.
  • TheIntroDB (theintrodb.org). Title identifiers, season or episode numbers and the media duration, used for intro and credits markers.
  • Image hosts. Poster and artwork requests go to TMDB, Trakt's image service or your media server.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect analytics, crash reports or usage statistics.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not run a server that stores your library, credentials or watch history.

Security

The iOS Keychain encrypts stored credentials. Quasar uses HTTPS when a server supports it. If you enter an http:// address, traffic to that server is not encrypted. The app allows cleartext connections because many home servers do not have a certificate. iOS asks for local network access only to reach a server on your local network.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove local data from the device. On iOS, Keychain items can remain as described above.
  • This version of the iOS app does not include a screen for removing a server or clearing the cache separately.

For data held by TMDB, Trakt or TheIntroDB, contact that service. For anything else, email us.

Children

Quasar is not directed to children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top will change. Material changes will be noted in the release notes.

隐私政策

适用于 iOS 和 iPadOS。生效日期 2026 年 10 月 18 日。版本 1.1。

Quasar 是个人媒体服务器的客户端，支持你自己运行或拥有账号的 Emby、Jellyfin 和飞牛影视服务器。

本政策适用于 iOS 和 iPadOS 版 Quasar。Android 版单独分发，功能更多。

Quasar 没有账号体系，也没有开发者运营的内容服务。我们不会收到你的媒体服务器密码，也不做统计和广告。

保存在设备上的信息

  • 媒体服务器地址、用户名和密码。存在 iOS 钥匙串里，只发送到你填写的服务器地址。
  • 随机设备号。首次启动时生成，发给你的媒体服务器用来识别这个客户端。它不是广告标识符，也不会发给我们。
  • 设置、观看进度、媒体库元数据、海报和播放缓存。

iOS 上删除 App 后，钥匙串里的条目可能还在。删除 App 可以清掉本地数据，钥匙串条目的情况见上面的说明。

联网发送的信息

请求从你的设备直接发出，不经过我们的服务器。

  • 你的媒体服务器。请求包含账号密码、设备号、媒体库查询、播放进度和观看状态。服务器运营方按他们自己的隐私政策处理这些数据。
  • TMDB（themoviedb.org）。片名搜索和元数据，TMDB 会收到你的 IP 地址。App 使用内置的 API 凭据，你不需要 TMDB 账号。
  • Trakt（trakt.tv）。发现和详情页用的公开榜单和海报，日历用的播出时间。Trakt 会收到你的 IP 地址。这个版本的 iOS App 不登录 Trakt，也不会把你的观看记录发给 Trakt。
  • TheIntroDB（theintrodb.org）。作品 ID、季集号和媒体时长，用来补片头片尾标记。
  • 图片服务器。海报和剧照来自 TMDB、Trakt 的图片服务或你的媒体服务器。

我们不做的事

  • 不收集统计、崩溃报告或使用数据。
  • 不投放广告，不使用广告标识符。
  • 不跨 App 或网站追踪你。
  • 不出售或出租个人数据。
  • 不运营保存你的媒体库、账号密码或观看记录的服务器。

安全

iOS 钥匙串会加密保存的账号密码。服务器支持时使用 HTTPS。如果你填的是 http:// 地址，到那台服务器的流量不加密。App 允许明文连接，因为很多家庭服务器没有证书。iOS 会请求本地网络权限，只用于连接局域网里的服务器。

你的选择

  • 删除 App 可以清掉设备上的本地数据。iOS 的钥匙串条目可能保留，见上面的说明。
  • 这个版本的 iOS App 还没有单独删除服务器或清理缓存的界面。

TMDB、Trakt、TheIntroDB 保存的数据，请找对应的服务。其他问题发邮件给我们。

儿童

Quasar 不面向 13 岁以下的儿童。13 岁以下请勿使用。

变更

App 变化时我们可能更新本政策，顶部的生效日期会变。重要变更会写进更新说明。
